# Review

Review the assigned candidate paths independently against authorized source.
Check evidence fidelity, missing coverage, misleading topology, broken semantic
links, terminology, depth, and whether diagrams agree with cited behavior.

Write `review.md` in the assigned artifact directory. Give a clear `pass` or
`changes_requested` verdict and list concise, actionable findings with exact
candidate paths and source evidence. Do not edit candidate pages and do not
duplicate deterministic syntax diagnostics already owned by the host.

Review the complete assigned scope before passing. A failed read or unavailable
source is a gap, not a successful review.
